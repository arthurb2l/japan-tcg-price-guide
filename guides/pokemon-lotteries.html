🎟️ How to Enter Pokémon Card Lotteries in Japan

Winning sealed Pokémon booster boxes at 定価 (list price) — the 抽選 system

Hot Pokémon boxes almost never sit on a shelf at list price. They sell by 抽選 (chūsen, lottery): apply in a window, a random draw picks winners, winners buy one box at 定価. A single product runs dozens of independent lotteries across retailers at once — each a separate draw you can enter — so the winning strategy is breadth. Anything "in stock" on a marketplace is a scalper at 2×.

🗾 Mini-glossary: 抽選 lottery · 当選 win / 落選 lose · 応募・エントリー apply · 定価 list price · 本人認証 identity verification · 購入履歴 purchase history · 整理券 numbered ticket · 先着 first-come.

⚡ The short version

  1. Pokémon Center Online is your #1 shot — do the My Number Card verification to get into the high-allocation lane (below).
  2. Then cast a wide net across the other free online lotteries — each is a separate draw.
  3. Front-load the odds-boosters (weeks ahead): AmiAmi mail-mag + purchase history (draw priority); Yodobashi / Joshin purchase history + rank (their gates thin the pool, ~16.9% at Yodobashi).
  4. File the free ones: Amazon 招待リクエスト (valid ~12 months) and 楽天ブックス, Toys"R"Us nationwide.

🪪 Does My Number (マイナンバー) help? — For Pokémon, YES

✅ On Pokémon Center Online, verification wins you the bigger pool. The official PCO 抽選 splits every product into two lanes: 「プレイヤーズクラブ：本人認証済み枠」 (identity-verified) and 「本人未認証枠」 — and the majority of the allocation goes to the verified lane. You get verified by completing 本人認証 in the Pokémon Card Game Players Club using a My Number Card (a separate account from your PCO shopping account). Requirements: verify before the application window closes, and your Players Club email must exactly match your PCO email (a dot/character difference drops you to the unverified lane). Then apply to the 認証済み枠 version of each product — if you're verified and apply to the 未認証枠 by mistake, you're auto-rejected (落選).
❌ Everywhere else — NO. Providing your My Number (the digits) does not increase odds at Rakuten, Amazon, Yodobashi, card shops, etc. By law My Number is restricted to tax / social-security tasks, not retail lotteries. Outside the PCO lane, the physical My Number card is only ever used as a photo-ID document (e.g. Bic Camera accepts it to stop multi-entry) — the number is irrelevant and doesn't raise odds.

💻 Online lotteries (定価)

Pokémon Center Online + Players Club

Official Verified lane ✔✔

Two accounts (PCO shopping + Players Club) linked by an identical email.
How: in Players Club do 本人認証 with a My Number Card → apply on PCO to the 本人認証済み枠 of each product.
Why #1: verified lane = majority of allocation. Limit 1/product/person; not first-come; past buyers of the same box excluded. Pay: card or konbini (unverified lane = card + 3D Secure only).

楽天ブックス (Rakuten Books)

Online

How: log in → tap 抽選エントリー (web, no app). ~2–4 day window; email to winners + マイページ. Card only; complete manually or it auto-cancels. Limit 1/household; some rounds need prior RB history. Rank ≠ odds.

Amazon.co.jp — 招待リクエスト

Online

How: on a box marked "招待された方のみ" sold by Amazon.co.jp, press 招待をリクエストする. No window — invites go out randomly; request valid ~12 months; win = email + ~48h buy link. 1/person; shared name/address risks cancel.

あみあみ (AmiAmi)

Online Odds booster ✔

How: 会員登録 → web form → buy if 当選. Real odds-booster: mail-magazine opt-in + qualifying AmiAmi purchase history give priority in the draw (Rakuten/Yahoo-shop history doesn't count). 1 box/person.

ヨドバシ.com

Online Pool-thinned ✔

Eligibility: account + purchase history (~2yr) or store history with point-card unification. Apply in a browser, ~2-day window. Reported win rate ~16.9% (gate thins the pool). Delivery or store pickup.

Joshin / ヤマダ / エディオン

App/Online

Joshin: Smile会員 rank gate (higher rank = higher-tier lottery; updates monthly).
ヤマダ: app only + store purchase history.
エディオン: SMS/電話認証 required; only some stores. All: pickup with ID, multi-account banned.

🏬 In store

Convenience stores — packs, not boxes

In store, conbini sell loose packs at 定価 (先着 on release morning, e.g. 5/person) and character くじ — not sealed boxes. Their e-commerce arms (セブンネット, ファミマオンライン) sometimes run web lotteries gated by order history.

Toys"R"Us / Babies"R"Us — nationwide 定価 lottery

Runs nationwide 抽選 at 定価 for hot Pokémon products (recently the 30th CELEBRATION card set) — one of the widest-reach 定価 routes. Apply online/in-store by the deadline; winners announced, then buy at release.

Electronics big-box stores (発売日)

Generally no open walk-in. Two per-store models announced on the store's 公式X: 整理券 (numbered ticket) → buy in order (getting it is 先着, queue early), or 事前抽選 → winners buy (gated to app members with history). Often card-only + shrink-less anti-resale.

Card shops

Lotteries, not first-come, 1 box/person, strict anti-multi-entry: カードラボ (当選メール → 店頭予約 w/ 内金), ホビーステーション (LivePocket, store pickup only, ID at pickup), 晴れる屋/2 (LivePocket), plus カードボックス, 竜星のPAO, 三洋堂, カードコレクト.
