🎟️ How to Enter TCG Lotteries in Japan

Winning sealed Pokémon & One Piece booster boxes at 定価 (list price) — the 抽選 system explained

In Japan, hot booster boxes almost never sit on a shelf at list price. They sell by 抽選 (chūsen, lottery): you apply in a window, a random draw picks winners, and winners buy one box at 定価. Anything "in stock" on a marketplace is usually a scalper reselling at 2×. This guide covers how to enter — online and in store — for both games (they run on the same retailer systems), and what actually improves your odds.

🗾 Mini-glossary: 抽選 lottery · 当選 win / 落選 lose · 応募・エントリー apply · 予約 preorder · 定価 list price · 会員 member · 購入履歴 purchase history · 整理券 numbered ticket · 先着 first-come · 本人確認 ID check.

⚡ The short version

  1. Cast a wide net across the rolling online lotteries — most are free, one entry per person, one click.
  2. Front-load the 3 things that genuinely move the needle (weeks ahead):
    • AmiAmi — opt into the mail-magazine + build AmiAmi purchase history → priority in the draw (the clearest real odds-booster).
    • Yodobashi / Joshin / Yamada — build purchase history / raise membership rank → their history gates thin the pool, so real odds beat open lotteries (Yodobashi ~16.9% reported).
    • Amazon 招待リクエスト — one-click "request invitation" on every eligible box; it stays valid ~12 months and costs nothing. Use Premium Bandai for One Piece.
  3. Verified identity never buys better odds — it only makes you eligible or stops multi-entry. Coverage + purchase history wins, not more verification.

🪪 Does My Number (マイナンバー) improve your chances?

It depends who runs the lottery — and this is the one place people get it wrong.

✅ Pokémon's own channel — YES, verification wins you the bigger pool. The official Pokémon Center Online 抽選 splits every product into two lanes: 「プレイヤーズクラブ：本人認証済み枠」 (identity-verified) and 「本人未認証枠」 (not verified) — and the majority of the allocation goes to the verified lane. You get verified by completing 本人認証 in the Pokémon Card Game Players Club using a My Number Card (a separate account from your shopping account). So here, doing the My Number-Card verification genuinely raises your chance of winning at 定価. Requirements: verify before the application window closes, and your Players Club email must exactly match your Pokémon Center Online email (a dot or character difference drops you to the unverified lane). Then apply to the 認証済み枠 version of each product — if you're verified and apply to the 未認証枠 by mistake, you're auto-rejected (落選).
❌ Third-party retailers — NO. Providing your My Number (the digits) does not increase odds at Rakuten, Amazon, Yodobashi, card shops, etc. — no evidence any of them use it for weighting, and by law My Number is restricted to tax / social-security / disaster tasks, not retail lotteries.
🪪 The card vs the number: outside Pokémon's verified lane, the physical My Number card is only ever used as a photo-ID document (like a licence) — e.g. Bic Camera accepts it at its in-store lottery to confirm identity and stop multi-entry. The number is irrelevant; the card just proves who you are, and it doesn't raise odds there.

Note: the Pokémon Center verified-lane example above is the current 30th CELEBRATION lottery (BOX ¥7,200 / FUTURISTIC BOX / 9-card set; apply 9/11–9/16, results 9/30, ships Jan 2027). Products and dates rotate every wave — the two-lane mechanism persists; reconfirm each round's terms. One Piece has no equivalent My-Number allocation lane (its official channel is Premium Bandai with SMS auth).

💻 Online lotteries (定価)

Both games use the same accounts/apps — the system doesn't change between Pokémon and One Piece. Windows, limits and dates change every release, so always reconfirm each event's 申し込み要項 (entry terms).

Pokémon Center Online + Players Club

Online Pokémon (official) Verified lane ✔✔

Two accounts: your PCO shopping account + a separate Players Club account, linked by an identical email.
How: in Players Club do 本人認証 with a My Number Card → apply on PCO to the 本人認証済み枠 of each product.
Why it's #1 for Pokémon: the verified lane holds the majority of the allocation. Unverified applicants get a small lane; if you're verified, only apply to the verified lane (else 落選).
Limit: 1/product/person; not first-come; past buyers of the same box excluded. Pay: card or konbini (unverified lane = card + 3D Secure only).

楽天ブックス (Rakuten Books)

Online Both

How: log in → tap 抽選エントリー on the product page. Web only, no app.
Window: ~2–4 days. Result: email to winners + マイページ (check spam).
Pay: credit card only (no コンビニ/代引); complete manually in the winner window or it auto-cancels.
Limit: 1 per household; some rounds need prior Rakuten Books purchase history. No SMS/ID. Membership rank does not change odds.

プレミアムバンダイ / プレバン

Online One Piece (primary)

How: free Bandai-ID 会員登録 → apply on the product page (~10-day window).
Note: One Piece lotteries require SMS / 電話認証 at application. Card authorization is taken at apply, finalized on win, released if you lose.
Result: email to winners. Limit: 1 per person. Payment method can't be changed after winning.

あみあみ (AmiAmi)

Online Both Odds booster ✔

How: 会員登録 → apply via web form in the window → buy if 当選. Credit / PayPal / 代引.
Real odds-booster: mail-magazine opt-in + qualifying AmiAmi purchase history give priority in the draw. (History from AmiAmi's Rakuten/Yahoo shops does not count.)
Limit: 1 box/person.

Amazon.co.jp — 招待リクエスト

Online Both

How: on a box marked "招待された方のみ" sold/shipped by Amazon.co.jp (not Marketplace), press 招待をリクエストする — that click is the entry.
No fixed window — invites go out randomly as stock lands; a request stays valid a set period (~12 months). Win = email + a ~48h buy link.
Limit: 1/person; shared name/address across accounts risks cancellation. "Prime wins more" is unverified.

ヨドバシ.com

Online Both Pool-thinned ✔

Eligibility: free account plus purchase history (past ~2yr) — net history, or store history with point-card unification done.
How: apply in a browser (app form may not render), ~2-day window. Win = emailed buy link + マイページ. Delivery or store pickup (unchangeable).
Reported win rate ~16.9% (the history gate thins the pool). Duplicate/same-name entries void.

Joshin (ジョーシン)

Online Both Rank gate ✔

Eligibility: Smile会員 rank gate — app path needs Silver+, web/mailmag path needs Gold/Platinum/VIP. Ranks are purchase-driven, update monthly on the 3rd.
How: マイページ → 店舗限定キャンペーンエントリー → pick product + pickup store. Some draws need ID at pickup (bring the device, screenshot invalid).

ヤマダ電機 (Yamada)

App Both

How: ヤマダデジタル会員 app only; requires store purchase history before the entry period + digital card in-store.
Apply: 店頭セール tab → lottery banner → pickup store. Win = app push (winners only). Pickup in person with code + ID within ~1 week. Exact payment/limit vary by event.

エディオン (Edion)

Online Both

How: Edion account; SMS / 電話番号認証 required (enter number → 受付コード → verify). Only some stores participate — check the store's 公式X / app / posters.
~2–3 day window. Win = SMS to winners. Pickup (win screen + member code + ID, no proxy) or delivery. Multi-account banned.

ビックカメラ (Bic Camera)

In-store only Both

No web/app lottery — application is in-store only. Notable: a My Number card (or other ID) is a required entry condition, used to prevent multi-entry (see above). Follow the store's 公式X for details.

🏬 In store

Convenience stores — packs, not boxes

In store, conbini sell loose packs at 定価 (先着 on release morning, e.g. 5 packs/person) and character くじ (merch draws) — not sealed 30-pack boxes. Their e-commerce arms (セブンネット, ファミマオンライン) have run web lotteries gated by purchase/order history, but whether those lots were sealed boxes or packs is unconfirmed. A Loppi/Famiポート terminal box-reservation flow is not evidenced — don't rely on it.

Electronics big-box stores on release day (発売日)

Flagships generally do not run open walk-in first-come sales for hot boxes. Two models, announced per-store on the store's 公式X:

  • 整理券 (numbered ticket) → buy in order — but getting the ticket is itself 先着; popular stores need queuing 1–2+ hours before open.
  • 事前抽選 (advance lottery) → winners buy on release — a random draw, usually gated to app members with purchase history.

Buyer steps: use each chain's 取扱店舗検索 to find participating Tokyo stores → build the required account/app and purchase history weeks ahead → follow the specific store's 公式X for ticket time / draw result → arrive very early if 整理券/先着, or appear in person with your win screen + member card + ID if 事前抽選. Expect credit-card-only at many stores, and shrink-less boxes as an anti-resale measure.

Card shops (カードラボ, ホビステ, 晴れる屋)

All run lotteries, not first-come, for hyped boxes — 1 box/person, strict anti-multi-entry:

  • カードラボ — 当選メール → 店頭予約 with a cash deposit (内金) → in-store purchase; ID at register. A re-lottery ran via X repost+follow (X account required).
  • ホビーステーション — WEB lottery via LivePocket (account required); one store per person; ~3-day window; win via QR in マイページ; store pickup only, no shipping; public ID required at pickup.
  • 晴れる屋 / 晴れる屋2 — box lottery via LivePocket in two lanes (通販 + 店舗); same one-box / one-account model.
