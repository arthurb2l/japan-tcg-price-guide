🎟️ How to Enter One Piece Card Lotteries in Japan

Winning sealed One Piece booster boxes at 定価 (list price) — the 抽選 system

Hot One Piece boxes almost never sit on a shelf at list price. They sell by 抽選 (chūsen, lottery): apply in a window, a random draw picks winners, winners buy one box at 定価. A single product runs many independent lotteries across retailers at once — each a separate draw — so the play is breadth. Anything "in stock" on a marketplace is usually a scalper above 定価 (and 24-pack listings risk being サーチ済 — rares pulled and resealed).

🗾 Mini-glossary: 抽選 lottery · 当選 win / 落選 lose · 応募・エントリー apply · 定価 list price · 電話番号/SMS認証 phone/SMS verification · 購入履歴 purchase history · 整理券 numbered ticket · 先着 first-come.

⚡ The short version

  1. Premium Bandai (プレバン) is your #1 shot — the primary official 定価 channel for One Piece boxes (requires SMS/phone verification).
  2. Then cast a wide net across the other free online lotteries — each is a separate draw.
  3. Front-load the odds-boosters (weeks ahead): AmiAmi mail-mag + purchase history (draw priority); Yodobashi / Joshin purchase history + rank (gates thin the pool, ~16.9% at Yodobashi).
  4. File the free ones: Amazon 招待リクエスト (valid ~12 months) and 楽天ブックス, plus card-shop lotteries.

💻 Online lotteries (定価)

プレミアムバンダイ / プレバン

Official (primary) Your #1 ✔

How: free Bandai-ID 会員登録 → apply on the product page (~10-day window).
Note: One Piece lotteries require SMS / 電話認証 at application. Card authorization is taken at apply, finalized on win, released if you lose. Result: email to winners. Limit 1/person. Payment method can't change after winning.

楽天ブックス (Rakuten Books)

Online

How: log in → tap 抽選エントリー (web, no app). ~2–4 day window; email to winners + マイページ. Card only; complete manually or it auto-cancels. Limit 1/household; some rounds need prior RB history. Rank ≠ odds.

Amazon.co.jp — 招待リクエスト

Online

How: on a box marked "招待された方のみ" sold by Amazon.co.jp, press 招待をリクエストする. No window — invites go out randomly; request valid ~12 months; win = email + ~48h buy link. 1/person. ⚠️ 3rd-party sellers = markup; 24-pack listings risk being サーチ品.

Online Odds booster ✔

How: 会員登録 → web form → buy if 当選. Real odds-booster: mail-magazine opt-in + qualifying AmiAmi purchase history give priority in the draw (Rakuten/Yahoo-shop history doesn't count). 1 box/person.

ヨドバシ.com / Joshin / 量販店

Online Pool-thinned ✔

🏬 In store

Convenience stores — packs, not boxes

In store, conbini sell loose packs at 定価 (先着 on release morning) — not sealed boxes. No reliable box-reservation flow at Loppi/Famiポート terminals.

Electronics big-box stores (発売日)

Generally no open walk-in. Two per-store models announced on the store's 公式X: 整理券 (numbered ticket) → buy in order (getting it is 先着, queue early), or 事前抽選 → winners buy (gated to app members with history). Often card-only + shrink-less anti-resale.

Card shops & events

Lotteries, not first-come, 1 box/person, strict anti-multi-entry: カードラボ, ホビーステーション (LivePocket, store pickup only, ID at pickup), 晴れる屋/2, カードボックス, 竜星のPAO. BANDAI CARD GAMES Fest (BCG Fest) events also run on-site box lotteries — see our release calendar for event dates.

❓ FAQ

Does giving my My Number improve my odds?

No. One Piece has no verified-allocation lane like Pokémon Center Online. The official channel, Premium Bandai, uses SMS / 電話番号認証 as an eligibility + anti-multi-entry check, not an odds booster. My Number never improves your One Piece odds anywhere; by law it's limited to tax / social-security use.

Is it the number or the card that matters?

Neither improves odds. The physical My Number card may be accepted as a photo-ID document at some in-store lotteries (like a licence) to confirm identity / stop multi-entry — the digits are never a weighting factor.

Is it first-come? Should I apply the moment it opens?

No — it's a random draw, not first-come. Applying early doesn't help; just apply before the deadline.

Can I enter the same box at several retailers?

Yes. Each retailer runs a separate, independent draw (one entry per retailer), so entering many raises your overall chance. That's the core strategy.

Are the "24-pack / 1 box" marketplace listings safe to buy?

Be careful. On Mercari and Amazon 3rd-party, cheap "24-pack" listings can be サーチ済 — the rare cards pulled and the box resealed. Prefer sealed テープ付 boxes from verified sources, or just win one at 定価 via the lotteries above.

Do I need a Japanese address?

Yes — winners ship to a Japan address, and most of these lotteries are Japan-only.
